# Final — Product, Impact, and Project Defense

**Type:** Team · **Due:** 2026-12-01 · **Points:** 100 · **Status:** Planned

## Purpose

Deliver the completed project and demonstrate what the team built, what evidence supports it, and
what impact it can create.

## Requirements

| Requirement | What Must Be Demonstrated | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-Quality End-to-End Solution | Deliver the complete agreed project scope with reliable Must Have functionality; demonstrate the full workflow from realistic input through processing to meaningful output and user value | 50% |
| Technical and Impact Evidence | Present final technical results, comparison with alternatives/baselines, user or stakeholder validation, and measurable industry/social impact — clearly distinguish demonstrated results from claims or future work | 30% |
| Final Demonstration and Project Defense | Clear live demonstration; every team member understands the architecture, major technical decisions, results, limitations, and their individual contribution; submit complete project documentation | 20% |

## Final deliverables

Working end-to-end system, GitHub repository, final report, deployment/user documentation,
presentation, live demonstration, short demo video (≤ 60 seconds), and individual contribution
statement.
