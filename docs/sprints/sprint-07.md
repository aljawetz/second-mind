# Sprint 7 — User/Stakeholder and Impact Validation

**Type:** Team · **Due:** 2026-10-20 · **Points:** 100 · **Status:** Planned

## Purpose

Determine whether the integrated system actually solves the intended problem and creates
measurable industry or social value.

## Requirements

| Requirement | What Must Be Demonstrated | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validation Design and Execution | Conduct an appropriate evaluation with target users, stakeholders, domain experts, representative data, or a defensible research evaluation design | 40% |
| Technical and Impact Results | Measure the previously defined success criteria (at least one technical metric, one user/business/social-impact metric); compare against current practice or another baseline where possible | 40% |
| Findings and Improvement Priorities | Interpret positive and negative findings, identify limitations, prioritize changes for the next iteration | 20% |

## Deliverable

3–5 page validation report plus supporting results.

## Relevant scope

Pilot courses named in [the design spec](../specs/2026-09-14-second-mind-design.md) §13 — 49797 (primary)
and 18654 (Software Testing and Operations, to prove the multi-course sidebar on different
content) — are the validation targets. §12's success metrics (citation groundedness, retrieval
precision@k, artifact groundedness, return usage, confidence) are what gets measured here.
